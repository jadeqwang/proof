A painting on cream xuan paper, wide 16:9, in one hand: fine-line baimiao ink outlines and transparent ink wash, the paper grain and a few long paper fibres showing through everywhere.

SUBJECT. An indoor, space-themed children's playground in Austin, Texas, in 2023, late in the afternoon. We are close to its big glass wall, looking straight at it: floor-to-ceiling plate glass in slim dark aluminium mullions, a low sill and a strip of padded foam-tile floor along the bottom. A low sun comes through the glass. Two people stand just in front of the glass, between us and the light, side by side and hand in hand, both in pure profile facing the left of the picture: a grandfather of about seventy and his grandson of about four. In the glass to the left of them is the faint reflection of the face of the woman whose eyes we are looking through: the boy's mother, the old man's daughter, aged 40.

COMPOSITION. Image 1 is the empty plate of this exact view: keep its glass, its two vertical mullions, its top rail, its sill and floor strip, and above all its diagonal band of light, exactly where they are, and add the people. The grandfather stands at the right, his whole head and body to the right of the right-hand mullion: he is tall in the frame, the top of his head near the top rail, and the bottom edge of the picture cuts him at the knee. The boy stands to the left of him, between the grandfather and the centre of the picture, and is small: the top of his big round head comes only a little above the grandfather's waist, and the bottom edge cuts him at mid-thigh. The boy's near arm is raised and his hand is held in the old man's lowered hand: the two joined hands are one small clear shape between the two bodies. The exact centre of the picture is empty lit paper: nothing is drawn there. The reflection of the woman's face is in the upper left pane, in the shaded part of the glass above the band of light: a head and the top of the shoulders only, about as large as the grandfather's head.

THE TWO FIGURES AGAINST THE LIGHT. They are seen against the sunlit glass, so each of them is one plain shape of deeper warm grey-gold wash, a soft silhouette with a clean brushed edge, noticeably darker than the wash on the glass but never black. Inside each shape there is nothing: no eye, no mouth, no nostril, no ear, no hair strands, no folds or buttons of clothing, no modelling. They are not portraits of anyone. All that tells us who they are is the outline: the old man a little stooped, slight, with thin short hair, a shirt collar, a loose cardigan and trousers; the boy with a big round head, a tuft of soft hair at the crown, a T-shirt, shorts and a child's short arms. What the outlines must show clearly is that the two are the same: they stand in the same way, perfectly still, and both heads are tipped forward and a little down by exactly the same few degrees, looking at the same spot ahead of them, absorbed, unaware of being watched. The same forehead, the same set of the chin, large and small.

HER REFLECTION. Her face is reflected faintly in the glass, in three-quarter view, turned toward the right of the picture. It is drawn in thin ink line only: no wash of its own and no colour, the wash of the glass and the mullion showing straight through it, as if it had been drawn on the glass with the very point of the brush. It is faint but complete and exact, and it is recognisably her. Her head is perfectly still. Her lips are together and level, her jaw loose, her brows resting. She is not smiling and not frowning and not posing for anyone: all the life in the face is in the eyes, which are fixed and bright on one tiny thing drifting in the air at the exact centre of the picture: both irises point to the right and slightly downward, at that same point. Do not draw the thing she is looking at. It is the same face as the two silhouettes: the same stillness, the same small forward tilt of the head.

LIGHT, the most important thing in the picture. Exactly as in image 1: the low sun comes through the glass as ONE broad, straight diagonal band from the upper right down to the lower left. Inside the band the glass is NOT PAINTED: bare cream paper left blank, with only the thin ink outlines of the mullions and sill. No yellow, no white paint, no glow, no rays, no haze, no bloom, no lens flare, no sun disc: light is simply where the brush did not go. Everything outside the band is under one even transparent warm grey-gold ink wash, quiet, with the lines showing through it. The edge between wash and blank paper is a clean, slightly soft wet edge. The two figures keep their deeper wash where they stand in front of the band: there is no bright outline round them and no light spilling onto them.

PEOPLE. Image 2 is the accepted face sheet of the woman in the reflection: reproduce THAT woman's face, but ten years older than on the sheet. Jade in 2023: a Chinese woman aged 40; an oval face with a softly pointed chin, almond eyes with deep near-black irises, dark, fairly strong, slightly asymmetric eyebrows (the eyebrow on the LEFT of the picture is straight; the eyebrow on the RIGHT of the picture runs straight and then lifts into a soft arch), a straight nose, full lips; at 40 the face is a little leaner, with a faint line at each side of the mouth and under each eye; her real, natural face, not idealised. Thin dark rectangular half-rim glasses. Long black hair with no fringe: an open forehead, parted slightly off-centre, in long soft layers with a loose wave, past the shoulders. Plain everyday clothes: the collar of a soft grey cardigan over a dark top. Ignore the white jacket on the sheet completely: no jacket, no orange band, no blue dot.
