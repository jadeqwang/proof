A painting on cream xuan paper, wide 16:9, in one hand: fine-line baimiao ink outlines and transparent ink wash, the paper grain and a few long paper fibres showing through everywhere.

SUBJECT. The unfinished basement of a house in Decatur, Illinois, in 1990, at night. Under her father's computer desk, in the dark of the knee space, a seven-year-old girl is being a cat: on her hands and feet, knees tucked up, back rounded, hands flat on the concrete like paws, her face toward us. We are on the floor at her level, looking in through the open end of the desk. Two wooden desk legs, the underside of the desk top, the side of the drawer pedestal behind her, a painted concrete-block wall at the left, a bare concrete floor; at the right her father's trouser leg and one leather lace-up shoe, and a spoke and caster of his rolling chair in a strip of light.

COMPOSITION. Image 1 is an earlier take of this exact painting. Repaint it as it is: the same camera, the same desk legs, desk top, panel, wall and floor, the same girl in the same crouch at the same size, the same arms and the same two hands with five spread fingers each, the same bare feet between them, the same hair, the same father's leg, shoe and chair caster at the right, the same strip of bare-paper light on the floor at the far right. Image 2 is the layout drawing the camera came from (a 3D model; do not keep its mechanical even line). Change only these three things.
ONE, her mouth. In image 1 she has a small smile: remove it. Her lips are together and perfectly LEVEL, the corners of the mouth neither up nor down, her jaw loose, her brows resting. She is not smiling and not frowning and not posing for anyone. She is perfectly content, and it shows only in how settled her body is and in her eyes, which are wide, bright and fixed on the caster of the chair, the way a cat watches a thing that might move: both irises turned toward the right side of the picture and a little downward, exactly as in image 1.
TWO, the dark. The only light in the room is a computer screen up on top of the desk; it cannot reach under the desk, so she is entirely in shade, and it is darker under here than image 1 shows. Lay ONE transparent wash of deep indigo (a blue-violet ink blue, clearly blue, not grey, not brown) over everything except the strip of light at the far right, in several soft flat layers, deepest up under the desk top and behind her, with slow cloudy variations and the paper grain breathing through; never flat black, never a solid fill. Under it nothing has a colour of its own: the desk legs and desk top are NOT brown wood, the shoe is NOT brown, the trousers are not grey: all are thin ink line under the same indigo wash. Flat wash and thin line, very little modelling. On her face and hands the wash is a thin glaze laid OVER normally painted skin: her warm rosy flesh colour and the pink blush on her cheeks still show clearly through it, never grey, never ashen. Her hair is glossy jet-black ink.
THREE, her eyes hold the only light on her: in each eye one small crisp catch-light of bare paper, a short low horizontal sliver (the lit floor beyond the desk), on the right side of each iris, the same in both eyes.

LIGHT. The strip at the far right, on the floor beyond the desk, across the chair's spoke and caster and the edge of the father's shoe, is NOT PAINTED: bare cream paper left blank with only thin ink outlines, as in image 1. No white paint, no glow, no rays, no haze, no bloom: light is simply where the brush did not go. The edge between the dark and the blank paper is a clean, slightly soft wet edge.

PEOPLE. THE GIRL. Little Jade at about age 7, Decatur, Illinois, 1990: a Chinese girl in first or second grade, with LARGE round dark eyes set wide apart, each with thick dark upper lashes; distinct, fairly strong, straight dark eyebrows; a tiny button nose; a small soft mouth; a round face with soft cheeks and a wide warm pink blush, the chin a touch longer than a small child's. Glossy jet-black hair (pure black ink, never brown): a light fringe of a few soft parted strands with the forehead showing between them, long side locks in front of the ears, a small half-ponytail tied high at the back of the crown with a dark band, the rest straight down to her shoulder blades. Clothes (1990 American Midwest, hand-me-down, plain): an oversized plain heather-grey crew-neck sweatshirt with the sleeves pushed up to the elbows, no print and no lettering; dark navy stirrup leggings; bare feet. Proportions of a seven-year-old: about five and a half heads tall, thin arms and legs, a big round head. She is 7: clearly older than 5 and clearly younger than 10.
