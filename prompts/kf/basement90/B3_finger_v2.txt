A painting on cream xuan paper, wide 16:9, in one hand: fine-line baimiao ink outlines and transparent ink wash, the paper grain and a few long paper fibres showing through everywhere.

SUBJECT. The unfinished basement of a house in Decatur, Illinois, in 1990, at night. A seven-year-old girl has hopped down from her father's chair and stands on the floor beside his desk, and with one small index finger she is touching the glass of his computer monitor, pointing at one exact place on the screen. We look over her shoulder from just behind and above her head. A bulky beige 1990 CRT monitor on a flat horizontal desktop computer case; its screen, a plain even blue with nothing on it, is the only light in the room. The back of the girl's head low in the middle; the back and side of her father's head and one shoulder at the right edge; an open box of floppy disks at the lower left; a stack of blank fan-fold paper behind the monitor; a painted concrete-block wall; a wooden desk top.

COMPOSITION. Image 1 is an earlier take of this exact painting. Repaint it as it is: the same camera, the same monitor and computer case, the same plain blue screen, the same small hand with its index fingertip on the glass in the same place, the same back of her head, the same father's head and shoulder at the right, the same disk box, paper stack, wall and desk. Image 2 is the layout drawing it was made from (a 3D model, composition only; do not keep its mechanical even line). Change only these three things.
ONE, the room is dark and the screen is its only light. In image 1 the room is evenly lit in a warm grey. Instead lay ONE even transparent wash of deep indigo (a blue-violet ink blue, clearly blue, not grey and not brown) over everything the screen light does not reach: the wall, the whole beige casing of the monitor and of the computer (line under indigo wash, no beige, no grey), the paper stack, the disk box and the far parts of the desk, the backs of both heads, her sweatshirt and his shirt. Soft transparent layers, a little deeper in the corners, the ink lines showing through and the paper grain breathing through; never flat black, never a solid fill. Flat wash and thin line, very little modelling.
TWO, the light is bare paper. Where the screen light falls the paper is left completely blank, with only thin outlines, no wash at all: her pointing hand and forearm; a narrow sliver along the curve of her cheek and ear and the top edge of her pushed-up sleeve, as seen from behind; a narrow sliver along her father's cheek, the rim of his ear and the top of his shoulder; and a soft-edged patch of the desk top and of the top of the computer case directly below and in front of the screen. No white paint, no glow, no rays, no haze, no bloom: light is simply where the brush did not go. The edge between wash and blank paper is a clean, slightly soft wet edge.
THREE, her hair and her father's hair are glossy jet-black ink (pure black, never brown). Hers is as on image 3: only a SMALL half-ponytail tied high at the back of the crown with a dark band, most of the hair hanging loose and straight down her back.

THE ONE THING SHE IS ATTENDING TO: the screen under her fingertip. The screen is the only object in the picture with a colour of its own: one flat, even, transparent wash of clear medium blue, the blue of a DOS screen, exactly as in image 1. It is completely plain: no text, no letters, no lines, no cursor, no reflections of the room. Against it, the centre of interest of the whole picture is her fingertip on the glass: the pointing hand is painted with the finest, most loving line in the picture and in warm rosy flesh colour on bare paper, each knuckle and the small nail drawn, the pad of the fingertip pressed flat on the glass with its small soft reflection, and a thin margin of clean bare paper round the hand and finger so that the hand seems lit from within. The index finger is straight; the other three fingers are curled into the palm; the thumb is tucked along the side.

PEOPLE. THE GIRL. Little Jade at about age 7, Decatur, Illinois, 1990: a Chinese girl in first or second grade, with LARGE round dark eyes set wide apart, each with thick dark upper lashes; distinct, fairly strong, straight dark eyebrows; a tiny button nose; a small soft mouth; a round face with soft cheeks and a wide warm pink blush, the chin a touch longer than a small child's. Glossy jet-black hair (pure black ink, never brown): a light fringe of a few soft parted strands with the forehead showing between them, long side locks in front of the ears, a small half-ponytail tied high at the back of the crown with a dark band, the rest straight down to her shoulder blades. Clothes (1990 American Midwest, hand-me-down, plain): an oversized plain heather-grey crew-neck sweatshirt with the sleeves pushed up to the elbows, no print and no lettering; dark navy stirrup leggings; bare feet. Proportions of a seven-year-old: about five and a half heads tall, thin arms and legs, a big round head. She is 7: clearly older than 5 and clearly younger than 10.
