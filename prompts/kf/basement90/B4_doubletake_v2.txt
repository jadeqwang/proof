A painting on cream xuan paper, wide 16:9, in one hand: fine-line baimiao ink outlines and transparent ink wash, the paper grain and a few long paper fibres showing through everywhere.

SUBJECT. The unfinished basement of a house in Decatur, Illinois, in 1990, at night. A father sits at his computer in a rolling office chair; the plain blue screen is the only light. A moment ago his seven-year-old daughter was standing at his elbow pointing at a mistake on the screen; he has fixed it and swung round to tell her so, and she is not there any more: she is under the desk, low at the left, crouched like a cat, looking out. A wooden desk against a painted concrete-block wall, a beige 1990 CRT monitor on a horizontal desktop computer case, a keyboard on a coiled cable, a box of floppy disks, a sheet of fan-fold paper, exposed floor joists overhead.

COMPOSITION. Image 1 is an earlier take of this exact painting. Repaint it as it is: the same camera, the same desk, monitor, plain blue screen, computer, keyboard, disk box and paper, the same seated man in the same pose with both hands over the keyboard and the steel watch on the same wrist, the same chair, the same wall and joists, the same patch of bare-paper light on the wall at the right, the same deep indigo wash everywhere else, and the same small girl under the desk with the same quiet face. Image 2 is the layout drawing it was made from (a 3D model, composition only; do not keep its mechanical even line). Change only these three things.
ONE, his age and likeness. In image 1 his face is drawn far too old, about fifty, with forehead lines and deep lines from nose to mouth. He is 37. Repaint his head as the man on image 3, which is his accepted head sheet at the right age: a smooth forehead with NO lines, NO lines from nose to mouth, no hollow cheeks, a lean young father's face with clear cheekbones and a softly pointed chin, thick jet-black hair (pure black, no grey), the same thin-metal square glasses with a double bridge. The bottom row of image 3 holds this moment: the bottom-left head is "Good catch!" and the bottom-middle head is the double take a second later. Paint the instant between them, keeping the turn of the head from image 1: eyebrows up, eyes wide and bright behind the glasses, mouth open on the last word of "Good catch!", pleased; and both irises aimed down and toward the left of the picture, at the height of a small child's face beside his chair, where there is now nobody.
TWO, the things in shade have no colour of their own. The desk is not brown and the monitor, computer and keyboard are not beige: they are thin ink line under the same transparent indigo wash as the wall, a little paler only where the screen light touches the keyboard, the front edge of the desk top, his hands and forearms, his face, throat, shirt front and lap: there the paper is left bare, with only thin outlines and, on his skin, his own warm flesh wash.
THREE, hair is glossy jet-black ink on both of them, never brown.

THE SCREEN is the one object with a colour of its own: one flat, even, transparent wash of clear medium blue, completely plain: no text, no letters, no lines, no cursor, nothing on it. In each of his eyes one tiny upright blue-white rectangular catch-light.

PEOPLE. THE FATHER. Dad, 1990: a Chinese man aged 37, slim and a little wiry; a long oval face with a softly pointed chin and clear cheekbones, a straight nose, near-black almond eyes, dark, fairly strong, slightly asymmetric eyebrows (one straight, one lifting into a soft arch), a thoughtful mouth; clean-shaven; thick jet-black hair side-parted and combed over, a little overdue for a cut, a few strands falling onto the forehead, ears showing; large thin-metal square-ish late-1980s glasses with a double bridge. A pale-blue short-sleeved button-up shirt with a chest pocket holding one pen, tucked into grey pleated trousers with a plain dark belt; a plain steel wristwatch on his left wrist; brown leather shoes. Image 3 is his accepted head sheet at the right age, 37: it is his identity, reproduce THAT man exactly (face, glasses, hair, shirt, age). A grown man and the father of a seven-year-old; no grey hair, no deep lines. THE GIRL, small, under the desk, exactly as in image 1: on hands and feet like a cat, lips together and level, eyes bright, her face a warm face under a deep indigo glaze, never grey.
