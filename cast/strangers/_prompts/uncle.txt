ONE person: an uncle in Shanghai, 1986; a lean Chinese man of about forty, a little stooped, sleeves rolled.
LEFT HALF of the sheet: three full standing figures of him, head to toe, the same scale, evenly spaced in one row: front view; three-quarter view; back view. In the three-quarter view both hands are raised in front of his chest, shaping something small.
RIGHT HALF of the sheet, detail studies floating on the bare paper with no frames. The LARGEST study, taking most of the right half: his two hands, drawn big and with great care, shaping a small figure out of pale flour dough: the thumb and first two fingers of one hand pinch and roll a pea-sized ball of dough, the other hand cradles the half-made little dough figure (a simple lump, not a recognisable character) on its palm; broad knuckles, short square nails, a little flour dust in the creases, a faded ink stain on one fingertip, a cheap steel wristwatch with a worn leather strap on the left wrist, shirt cuffs rolled twice. Smaller studies beside it: (2) the same hands a moment later, opening, palm up, offering the tiny ball, as if explaining; (3) the texture of his shirt close up; (4) his shoes as a pair, three-quarter view; (5) his feet seen from above, standing, toes turned slightly outward, weight on the left foot.
COSTUME: a short-sleeved-length rolled white cotton shirt gone faintly grey-blue from washing, of thin "diqueliang" polyester-cotton with a fine woven self-stripe, soft pointed collar, top button open, a fountain pen clipped in the chest pocket, the edge of a white cotton vest showing at the neck; high-waisted loose grey-blue trousers with a pressed crease and a thin brown belt, cuffs a little short; pale grey nylon socks; black cloth shoes with white stitched soles (Beijing-style cloth slip-ons), dusty at the toe. Short plain black hair with a side parting, shown only as light line.
