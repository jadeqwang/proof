LEFT TWO-THIRDS of the sheet: three full standing figures of her, head to toe, the same scale, evenly spaced in one row: front view, both hands resting on the handle of a small wire shopping cart that stands in front of her, patient; three-quarter view, with the cart; back view, without the cart, handbag hanging from the crook of her left elbow.
RIGHT THIRD of the sheet, detail studies larger than the figures, floating on the bare paper with no frames: (1) the cloth of her coat close up with one large button and the edge of the headscarf; (2) her two hands folded one over the other on the cart's handle bar: thin skin, raised veins in a few fine lines, a plain thin gold ring, a folded paper tissue tucked in the cuff; (3) her shoes as a pair, three-quarter view; (4) her feet seen from above, standing still, set neatly side by side, toes pointing straight ahead, with the front wheel of the cart; (5) her handbag.
COSTUME: a knee-length wool car coat in soft heather lavender-grey with a nubbly bouclé texture, raglan shoulders, three large round mother-of-pearl-grey buttons, a little too big for her; under it the collar of a pale blue cardigan; a thin printed headscarf in faded sage green with a small white dot, tied under the chin (the headscarf, being above the collar, is drawn only in pale thin line with the faintest sage tint and its dots; it frames the empty face; a little hair shows at the front as two or three pale lines, with no grey fill); a straight brown tweed skirt to below the knee; thick beige support stockings, a little wrinkled at the ankle; cream-white orthopaedic lace-up walking shoes with thick crepe soles; a boxy dark brown leather handbag with a brass twist clasp and short stiff handles. In the cart: a paper grocery bag, a bunch of celery, one tin, nothing with a label.
HEAD, IMPORTANT: the headscarf must NOT be painted in solid colour. It is part of the unfinished area: only its outline, its knot under the chin and four or five of its dots are drawn, in pale thin grey line on bare paper, with no green wash at all above the coat collar (the scarf's colour appears ONLY in the detail study of the coat collar, where the two knotted scarf ends lie on the coat). So the whole head, scarf, hair and face, is the same bare cream paper as the background, held by a few light lines; it must never look like a dark hood round a white hole.
