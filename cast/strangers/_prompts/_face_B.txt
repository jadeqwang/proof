THE FACE HAS NO FEATURES (most important rule, applies to every head on the sheet, at every size): the head is fully painted like the rest of the figure: hair in soft ink wash, and the face, ears and neck carry the same warm even flesh wash as the hands, with a normal ink outline for jaw and ear. But inside the face nothing is drawn: no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose, no mouth, no nostrils, no lips, no wrinkles, no shading that suggests features. A calm, smooth, plain flesh-coloured area where the features would be.
