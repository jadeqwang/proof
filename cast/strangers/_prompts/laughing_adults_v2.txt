FOUR different adult relatives in a room in Shanghai, 1986, laughing together, drawn as a small child of three sees them from the floor: a LOW upward angle, so legs, hems, hands and shoes are large and near and the heads are small, far away and high up.
LEFT HALF of the sheet: the four adults standing in a loose half-circle, full figures seen from knee height looking up, foreshortened; bodies rocking with laughter: one bent forward with a hand on a knee, one leaning back, one with a hand flapping, one wiping an eye with a knuckle (the fist covers part of the empty face; there is no eye under it).
RIGHT HALF of the sheet, detail studies floating on the bare paper with no frames, as large as the group: a row of the four pairs of lower legs and shoes side by side from trouser hem or skirt hem down, seen from the front at floor level, each pair pointing a different way (two pairs toward each other, one pair toward the viewer, one pair half turned away to the door); then four studies of hands: a hand slapping a thigh, a hand holding a lidded enamel tea mug, a hand with a folding paper fan, a hand pinching a cigarette-length stick of sugarcane; and two close studies of cloth: a small floral print and a grey-blue cotton twill.
THE FOUR: (a) an older aunt in a short-sleeved cotton blouse printed with small indigo flowers on white, loose black trousers ending above the ankle, black cloth strap shoes with a button, white ankle socks, holding a folding fan; (b) a tall uncle in a white sleeveless cotton vest tucked into grey-blue twill trousers with a brown belt, trouser legs rolled once, brown plastic sandals over bare feet, holding a white enamel mug with a lid; (c) a young aunt in a pale yellow dacron dress with a small collar and a hem just below the knee, nylon knee-socks in flesh pink, white plastic sandals with a low heel; (d) an old grandfather in a grey Zhongshan-style cotton jacket buttoned to the neck, wide dark trousers, black cloth shoes with white soles, one hand on a bamboo cane.
HEADS: all four are Chinese adults of 1986 with straight hair, shown only as a few pale open lines: (a) a short straight bob tucked behind the ears; (b) a short flat crew cut; (c) two short low plaits; (d) thin hair combed straight back. No curls. Because the heads are far up and tipped back with laughter, draw each one as nothing more than a pale open jaw line and hair line: NOT ONE mark inside any face: no closed-eye line, no lash line, no laughing mouth, no nostril, no cheek crease. The laughter is shown only by the bodies and hands.
