THE FACE IS NOT DRAWN (most important rule, applies to every head on the sheet, at every size): the drawing gets less and less finished as it goes up the figure. Shoes, clothes, hands and everything held are fully inked and washed. At the collar the colour wash stops. Above the collar everything is only a few pale, thin, broken grey construction lines on bare paper: one line for the neck, one open broken line for the jaw and chin that does not close, a loose light line for the outer shape of the hair with NO ink fill and NO wash (the hair is bare paper too), the lines fading out and stopping before the top of the head. Inside the face there is ONLY bare cream paper, the same as the background: no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose, no mouth, no shading, no flesh wash, no hint of features. It must look like a drawing the painter simply has not finished yet, calm and ordinary, never like a mask, a mannequin or a ghost.
