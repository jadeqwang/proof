THE FACE IS NOT DRAWN (most important rule, applies to every head on the sheet, at every size): this is an unfinished study in which the painter finished the clothes, hands and shoes and never reached the face. Inside the outline of each face there is ONLY bare cream paper, exactly the same colour as the background: no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose, no mouth, no nostrils, no lips, no wrinkles, no shading, no flesh wash, no dots, no faint suggestion of features. The jaw, chin, ear and hairline are drawn in a pale, thin, light grey line, lighter than the lines of the clothes. Hair is only a light open line sketch with a very pale wash. The neck and hands DO have their normal warm flesh wash; the wash stops softly below the jaw. It must look calm and unfinished, like an underdrawing left for later, never like a mask, a mannequin, a ghost or a smooth egg-shaped head.
