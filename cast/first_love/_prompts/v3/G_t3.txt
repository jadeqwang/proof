An over-the-shoulder study sheet for ONE young man who is only ever seen from behind: six studies of his head, neck and shoulders, in two rows of three, well separated, each floating on bare paper with soft vignetted edges, no panel, no box, no hard rectangular edges. These are the exact camera angles the film uses on him; he is always in the near foreground, back to us, and the drawing is about his HAIR. TOP ROW, the three shots as framed, his head and shoulders large in the frame at one side, the rest of the frame a pale washed hint of the setting: (1) grad school, aged about 24: from behind his right shoulder, slightly above his eye level, he leans forward toward a floral-upholstered couch by a window (only a pale suggestion of the couch and window, NO person drawn), holding out in both hands a folded WHITE sweatshirt with a round DARK-BLUE seal printed on it; we see the back and right side of his head, the curtain fringe tip at his temple and the crisp side line running back from it above his right ear, his right ear and the line of his jaw from behind, NO eye, nose or mouth; both his hands hold the sweatshirt correctly from below, thumbs on top; (2) aged 27: from behind and to his left, he stands at a dark espresso cube bookshelf with books, his head bowed and tilted down to his RIGHT as if leaning in to whisper to someone shorter beside him at his right (that person NOT drawn, out of frame), his face turned away from us; his arms relaxed at his sides; we see the top of his head and the centre part, the crisp side line above his left ear, the clean nape; (3) aged 19: from high behind, he sits cross-legged on the floor of a dim closet, back slightly rounded, head bowed toward his hands in his lap, a narrow shaft of light from a door gap beyond him; we look almost straight down on the TOP of his bowed head: the fringe and front hairline at the far side, the clean centre part running from there to the subtle crown point at the top of the skull and STOPPING there, the back of the head below it with NO parting, the straight fall of the fringe to both sides, the nape. BOTTOM ROW, the same three angles in the same order, cropped tight on the head and neck alone, no setting, drawn large and precisely: (4) the grad-school angle; (5) the bookshelf angle; (6) the closet angle: a BIRD'S-EYE view looking almost straight DOWN on the top of his bowed head, so the top of the skull fills the study: the front hairline and the fringe at the TOP of the picture, the centre part running from it DOWN the picture to the quiet crown point at the CENTRE of the study, and below the crown point (toward us) the back of the head with NO parting, the hair smooth and unbroken, only a sliver of the nape and the hoodie collar at the bottom. The haircut is IDENTICAL in all six studies.
