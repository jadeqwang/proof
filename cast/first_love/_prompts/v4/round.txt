MOST IMPORTANT CORRECTION, "LESS ROUND": in the straight-behind studies (1) and (4) the hair must NOT form a dome, a ball, a helmet or a bowl with a smooth circular rim. Instead: the top is gently FLATTENED where it lies on either side of the centre part; from the temples the hair drops nearly VERTICALLY, close to the skull, so the outline is TALLER THAN WIDE, a slim, softly squared shape barely wider than the head itself; the back is NOT one smooth shell but lies in flat, straight, separate layered strands whose ends form a slightly uneven, textured, point-cut edge over the short nape (not a perfect curve). The overall silhouette from behind looks more like a softened upright rectangle or a narrow shield than a circle.
