THE SWEATSHIRT: a plain WHITE crew-neck cotton fleece sweatshirt (ribbed collar, cuffs and waistband, set-in sleeves, slightly worn and soft) printed in a single DARK NAVY BLUE ink with the seal of the Institute for Advanced Study, drawn by hand in the same painting style (a hand-painted rendering of a printed design, not a photograph of a logo). The seal is ENORMOUS: a circle about 40 to 45 cm across that fills almost the ENTIRE FRONT of the sweatshirt, from just under the collar ribbing nearly down to the waistband ribbing and nearly from side seam to side seam; under it, near the hem, one line of thin, widely spaced sans-serif capitals, INSTITUTE FOR ADVANCED STUDY. The seal design, all in thin navy line on white like an engraved medal: one thin outer circle; inside it, two classical Greek women standing side by side on a short ground line, full length, holding hands between them; behind them a leafy fruit tree whose rounded crown of leaves and small round fruits fills the upper part of the circle. On the LEFT stands TRUTH, a slender classical figure in the plain, smooth, simplified outline of an antique marble statue, a light veil of drapery hanging from her shoulder down across her body to her knees, holding up a small round hand MIRROR on a handle in her raised right hand beside her head; on the RIGHT stands BEAUTY, in a long, flowing, finely pleated Greek gown (a chiton) to her feet, her left arm raised and curved over the top of her head, her long hair loose over one shoulder. The word TRUTH runs in widely spaced capitals up the inside of the left edge of the circle (reading from bottom to top), and the word BEAUTY runs down the inside of the right edge (reading from top to bottom). The ground line forms a small flat band across the bottom of the circle. No other words, no year, no Latin. Because the seal spans the whole front, folding the sweatshirt hides most of it: the whole seal can only be seen when the sweatshirt is opened out flat.
