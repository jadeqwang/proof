MOST IMPORTANT CORRECTION, "A STEP, NOT A FADE": the director says the previous drawing's change from the long top to the short lower hair was TOO GRADUAL. In every study the hair must read as two separate halves: a long, glossy, straight top layer, and under it a lower half of EVEN, short (1.5 to 3 cm), matte, grown-out buzz, the same length all over, from the tops of the ears down to the nape. Between them a crisp, visible step or ledge where the top layer's ends overhang the short hair, with a thin line of shadow under the overhang. Do NOT taper or fade the lower section toward the neck, do NOT shave it to skin, do NOT blend the two halves.
