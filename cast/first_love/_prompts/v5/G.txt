An over-the-shoulder study sheet for ONE young man who is only ever seen from behind: six studies of his head, neck and shoulders, in two rows of three, well separated, each floating on bare paper with soft vignetted edges, no panel, no box, no hard rectangular edges. These are the exact camera angles the film uses on him; he is always in the near foreground, back to us, and the drawing is about his HAIR. TOP ROW, the three shots as framed, his head and shoulders large in the frame at one side, the rest of the frame a pale washed hint of the setting: (1) grad school, aged about 24: from behind his right shoulder, slightly above his eye level, he leans forward toward a floral-upholstered couch by a window (only a pale suggestion of the couch and window, NO person drawn), holding out in both hands a neatly folded WHITE HOODIE (white drawstrings and the edge of the hood showing at one end of the fold), on the folded top its chest print: a small round BLUE filled-disc seal, the design in white reverse, as described below; both his hands hold the hoodie correctly from below, thumbs on top; (2) aged 27: from behind and to his left, he stands at a dark espresso cube bookshelf with books, his head bowed and tilted down to his RIGHT as if leaning in to whisper to someone shorter beside him at his right (that person NOT drawn, out of frame), his face turned away from us;
