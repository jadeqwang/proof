HIS HAIR, the thing that identifies him from behind (follow it exactly, the same cut and length at every age): DARK BROWN, a deep natural brown that reads clearly brown and never black, with a soft warm-brown sheen where the light catches it. Fine, STRAIGHT and smooth, lying sleek and close: NO waves, NO curls, NO shag, NO tousled or choppy spikes, nothing flipping up. The cut is a present-day men's curtain-fringe cut. On top: medium length, about 10 to 12 cm, cut in soft straight layers that fall forward and outward from a small crown whorl in fine straight comb-lines. At the front: a centre or slightly off-centre parting, the fringe falling to either side of the forehead in two soft straight curtains that end near the outer ends of the brows and sweep back toward the temples. Sides and nape NEAT and TAPERED: underneath, a short two-block undercut, clipped short (1 to 2 cm) on the lower sides, behind the ears and at the nape, with the longer straight top layer hanging over it, so that a soft straight hem shows where the long top hair ends above the short dark under-section. The top layer at most just covers the tops of the ears; the rest of each ear is visible. The nape is tapered short and clean to a soft natural hairline well above the collar: nothing reaches the collar, nothing curls at the nape. Seen from straight behind: a smooth rounded cap of straight dark-brown hair with straight strand-lines radiating down from the crown whorl, a straight soft hem across the back of the head at about the height of the ear tops, and below it the short dark taper and the clean nape. Seen from three-quarter behind: the tip of the curtain fringe shows past the temple and the brow, a straight forward-and-down sweep separate from the side layer, and the short clipped side shows under the overhang above the ear.
He must be easy to tell apart, from behind, from another young man in the same film (not on this sheet) who has short, choppy, tousled JET-BLACK hair with no fringe showing and his ears fully exposed: this man's hair is brown, longer and smooth on top, parted, with curtain bangs and a two-block taper.
