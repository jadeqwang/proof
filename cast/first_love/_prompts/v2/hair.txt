HIS HAIR, the thing that identifies him from behind (follow it exactly, the same cut and length at every age): DARK BROWN, a deep espresso / dark-chocolate brown, darker than chestnut, that still reads as brown and never as black, with a soft brown sheen where the light catches it. Fine, STRAIGHT and smooth, lying sleek and close: NO waves, NO curls, NO shag, NO tousled or choppy spikes, nothing flipping up. The cut is a present-day men's curtain-fringe cut. On top: medium length, about 10 to 12 cm, cut in soft straight layers that fall forward and outward from a small crown whorl in fine straight comb-lines. At the front: a centre or slightly off-centre parting, the fringe falling to either side of the forehead in two soft straight curtains that end near the outer ends of the brows and sweep back toward the temples. Sides and nape NEAT and TAPERED: underneath, a short two-block undercut on the lower sides and behind the ears, clipped short (1 to 2 cm); the longer straight top layer falls over it with soft, feathered, point-cut ends, so the overhang is SUBTLE and blends, not a blunt ledge. At the back the layers are graduated: shorter and shorter down the back of the head, blending smoothly into the short tapered nape. NOT a bowl cut, NOT a mushroom cut, NOT a helmet with a hard circular rim. The top layer at most just covers the tops of the ears; the rest of each ear is visible. The nape is tapered short and clean to a soft natural hairline well above the collar: nothing reaches the collar, nothing curls at the nape. Seen from straight behind: soft volume at the crown, straight separated strand-lines radiating down from the crown whorl in light layers with feathered tips, the layers stepping down and blending into the short dark taper and the clean nape; at the sides the top layer's feathered ends just brush the tops of the ears. Seen from three-quarter behind: the tip of the curtain fringe shows past the temple and the brow, a straight forward-and-down sweep separate from the side layer, and the short clipped side shows faintly under the feathered side layer above the ear.
He must be easy to tell apart, from behind, from another young man in the same film (not on this sheet) who has short, choppy, tousled JET-BLACK hair with no fringe showing and his ears fully exposed: this man's hair is brown, longer and smooth on top, parted, with curtain bangs and a two-block taper.
