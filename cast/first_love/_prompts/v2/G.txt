An over-the-shoulder study sheet for ONE young man who is only ever seen from behind: six studies of his head, neck and shoulders, in two rows of three, well separated, each floating on bare paper with soft vignetted edges, no panel, no box, no hard rectangular edges. These are the exact camera angles the film uses on him; he is always in the near foreground, back to us, and the drawing is about his HAIR. TOP ROW, the three shots as framed, his head and shoulders large in the frame at one side, the rest of the frame a pale washed hint of the setting: (1) grad school, aged about 24: from behind his right shoulder, slightly above his eye level, he leans forward toward a floral-upholstered couch by a window (only a pale suggestion of the couch and window, NO person drawn), holding out in both hands a folded WHITE sweatshirt with a round DARK-BLUE seal printed on it; we see the back and right side of his head, the curtain fringe tip past his temple, his right ear and jaw line; (2) aged 27: from behind and to his left, he stands at a dark espresso cube bookshelf with books, head bowed toward his own raised LEFT forearm (the cord bracelet on that wrist, dark forearm hair), so that we see the crown, the straight layers falling forward, the fringe hanging toward his eye, the clean tapered nape; (3) aged 19: from high behind, he sits cross-legged on the floor of a dim closet, back slightly rounded, head bowed toward his hands in his lap, a narrow shaft of light from a door gap beyond him; we see the crown whorl from above, the parting, the straight fall of the fringe to both sides and the nape. BOTTOM ROW, the same three angles in the same order, cropped tight on the head and neck alone, no setting, drawn large and precisely: (4) the grad-school angle; (5) the bookshelf angle; (6) the closet angle from above. The haircut is IDENTICAL in all six studies.
