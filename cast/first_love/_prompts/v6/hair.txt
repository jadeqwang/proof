HIS HAIR, the thing that identifies him from behind (follow it exactly, the same cut and length at every age): a deep warm DARK-CHOCOLATE BROWN, like 60-70% dark chocolate: a warm, rich dark brown, clearly brown in light, never black, not auburn or red, with a soft sheen. Fine and basically STRAIGHT (no curls, no waves, no spikes, nothing flipping up). A present-day male K-pop idol's curtain-fringe cut in TWO DISTINCT HALVES, a long TOP LAYER over a short LOWER SECTION:
- PART: a narrow CENTRE parting on TOP of the head only, from the middle of the forehead hairline straight back to the crown point at the very top of the skull, where it STOPS. From behind it shows at most as a tiny SHORT NOTCH in the outline at the very top of the head, on the centre line; NO line of scalp running down the back of the head. The fringe falls from it in two curtains to either side of the forehead, sweeping back toward the temples.
- THE TOP LAYER: long, straight, lying fairly close, its lower edge running from the fringe tip at each temple back along the side of the head just above the top of the ear, and around the back of the head at about the level of the tops of the ears. Its ends hang free as a slightly uneven, point-cut EDGE.
- THE LOWER SECTION: everything below the top layer (over and behind the ears, the lower back of the head and the nape) is a GROWN-OUT BUZZ: an EVEN 1.5 to 3 cm of short, soft, slightly fuzzy dark-brown pile, the same length all over, NOT tapered, NOT faded, NOT shaved; a separate, flatter, matte, slightly darker block.
- THE STEP: where the two halves meet there is a clear, visible STEP: the long top layer OVERHANGS the short lower section like a lid, with a thin line of shadow under it. NOT a fade, NOT a blended taper.
- SILHOUETTE, NOT ROUND: NOT a dome, NOT a ball, NOT a mushroom or helmet: the top layer lies close and fairly flat from the part and hangs nearly straight; below the step the lower section follows the skull closely, narrower than the top layer.
- TEXTURE, LIVED-IN (the director's note for this version): his hair must NOT look freshly combed, blow-dried or salon-perfect. He doesn't brush it every day: he just runs his fingers through it and goes. So the top layer is FINGER-COMBED: it falls in loose, slightly separated PIECES and soft clumps of strands rather than one smooth glossy sheet; the comb-lines are irregular, a few strands lie across their neighbours or slightly out of line, the surface has a gentle natural texture and a little air in it, the gloss broken up into separate highlights on the pieces, and the point-cut ends are a little uneven and piecey. The part is a natural, slightly irregular finger-made part (not a sharp comb-drawn line), still at the same place. It is still TIDY overall: NOT messy, NOT tousled, NOT bed-head, NOT spiky, NOT shaggy, NOT windblown; the cut, length, step and outline are exactly the same, only the surface is lived-in.
The top layer at most just covers the tops of the ears; the rest of each ear is visible against the short lower section.
He must be easy to tell apart, from behind, from another young man in the same film (not on this sheet) who has short, choppy, tousled JET-BLACK hair with no fringe showing and his ears fully exposed: this man's hair is brown, longer on top, centre-parted, with curtain bangs, over a distinct short grown-out buzz below.
