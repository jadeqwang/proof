LAYOUT, identical in every plate of this series. A single plant, centred left to right, standing on a small low mound of bare earth drawn in fine engraved hatching with three or four tiny pebbles; the top of the mound, where the plant's stem leaves the earth, is exactly at the horizontal centre of the sheet and 76% of the way down from the top. The mound is about 12% of the sheet's width. Below the mound, centred, at about 88% down, a small engraved caption in two lines: "CYNARA SCOLYMUS." in small spaced roman capitals, and under it in smaller copperplate italic "Globe Artichoke." Everything else is generous empty paper margin.
